Review this as a lead checking work that a mixed-skill team will use — most members have no scripting background. Standard: if a teammate can't follow it on first read, it failed. Flag: steps that assume scripting knowledge, error messages no non-technical user could act on, anything that requires calling the author. The author tends to skip steps that feel obvious to them. Look for that specifically.
