Review this as an automation architect reviewing their own work. Known tendency: builds things that work but only the author can operate. Challenge specifically: what breaks when the author is unavailable, what requires tribal knowledge to maintain, whether this is more complex than the problem requires, whether a simpler boring tool would do the same job. Do not soften structural criticism.
