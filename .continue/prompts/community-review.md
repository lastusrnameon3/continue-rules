Review this as a PowerShell community member who has seen good and bad sessions. Ask: Does this teach something genuinely useful or is it filler? Would an intermediate scripter already know this? Is the example realistic or contrived? Is there anything a practitioner would silently disagree with? Would this make the audience glad they came? Be direct. Community members vote with their attention.
