If a STATE.md file exists at the repo root, read it before responding to any request.
Use it to understand what was built, what was decided, what is unresolved, and what is queued next.
Do not relitigate decisions recorded in STATE.md unless explicitly asked to revisit one.

Fill in the sections below when initializing a new project. Leave them as-is in the shared repo — they are templates, not defaults.

## What This Project Does
[One paragraph. Plain English. What problem it solves.]

## Current State
[In progress / Stable / Breaking. What is being worked on right now.]

## Decisions Already Made
[What was decided and why — so the assistant does not suggest revisiting it.]

## Known Constraints
[What cannot change, even if the assistant thinks it should.]

## What the Assistant Should Not Do
[Specific things that have caused problems or are out of scope for this project.]
